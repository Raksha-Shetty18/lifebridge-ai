01. The Problem

The "Action Gap" in Generative AI

🚨 The Real-World Crisis
  • • Fragmented portals, confusing prerequisites & hidden guidelines.
  • • High anxiety & decision fatigue during stressful incidents.
  • • Strict time windows (e.g. scholarship appeals, fraud alerts).
  • • No centralized way to track multi-day progress toward resolution.
⚠️ Why Traditional Chatbots Fail
  • • Produces walls of 15 unranked bullet points.
  • • Fails to tell you: "What should I do right now today?"
  • • Ephemeral: closes the tab and everything is lost.
  • • Hallucinates broken links, outdated rules, and fake phone numbers.
